## Overview

AgriLink is an IoT-based system designed to reduce post-harvest spoilage among smallholder farmers storing fruit-bearing crops. By monitoring real-time storage conditions, alerting farmers to spoilage risks, and providing buyers with verified handling data, AgriLink bridges the trust gap between farmers and the market — increasing income, reducing waste, and stabilizing food supply.

> Philippines loses an estimated **₱35 Billion** in fresh produce annually due to post-harvest spoilage. Smallholder farmers lose 20–40% of fruit-bearing harvests because of uncontrolled storage conditions and a lack of early warning tools.

---

## The Problem

- Produce is stored under uncontrolled heat and humidity right after harvest
- Farmers have no tools to detect early spoilage triggers
- Buyers cannot verify handling quality, leading to distrust and lower selling prices
- Result: **lower income → higher waste → unstable market supply**

---

## The Solution

AgriLink addresses the supply chain gap through a four-step system:

| Step | Feature | Description |
|------|---------|-------------|
| 1 | **Sense** | Low-cost sensors monitor real-time temperature and humidity inside storage areas |
| 2 | **Alert** | Instant SMS alerts notify farmers and cooperatives when conditions increase spoilage risk |
| 3 | **Verify** | A Handling Quality Score shows buyers how well the produce was stored |
| 4 | **Sell** *(Optional)* | A B2B transparency marketplace allows coops and middlemen to source verified batches |

---

## Key Features

**Smart Storage Sensors**
- Non-contact temperature and humidity monitoring using low-cost ESP32 + SHT31 hardware

**SMS Alerts for Any Phone**
- No smartphone or app required — fast, low-cost warnings sent directly to farmers

**Handling Quality Score**
- A transparent record of storage conditions tracked since harvest

**B2B Transparency Platform**
- Buyers can browse and source verified produce batches

**QR Batch Tagging**
- Every batch receives a digital profile accessible to buyers via QR code

---

## Scaling Features

- **Predictive Shelf-Life Risk Modeling** — AI-driven analysis of storage patterns to forecast spoilage risk
- **Cold-Chain & Logistics Integration** — Partner facilities can attach their own handling data
- **Multi-language + Offline First** — Tagalog language support
- **Insurance & Government Integration** — Verified handling data for PCIC risk scoring

---

## Target Market

**Phase 1 – Beachhead Market**
Small to medium farmers under cooperatives storing fruit-bearing crops (durian, rambutan, lanzones, mango, etc.)

**Phase 2 – Expansion**
Cooperatives, traders, and aggregators handling bulk storage

**Phase 3 – Ecosystem Integration**
Exporters, cold-chain operators, insurers (PCIC), and Local Government Units (LGUs)

---

## Revenue Model

1. **Cooperative Subscriptions** — Dashboard access and sensor network support
2. **Middleman Premium Access** — Verified batches, handling scores, and early alerts
3. **Data / API Monetization** — Insights for PCIC, LGUs, and exporters
4. **Marketplace Visibility Fees** — Promoted batch listings (not commissions)
5. **Insurance Revenue Share** — Risk scoring reduces claims; shared savings model
